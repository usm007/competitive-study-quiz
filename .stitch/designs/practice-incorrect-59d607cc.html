The Constitution of India was adopted on:

A
26 November 1949
✓ CORRECT
B
26 January 1950
✕ YOURS
C
15 August 1947
D
26 November 1950
NOT QUITEA. 26 November 1949
YOUR ANSWER BeastCORRECT ANSWER A

KEY DISTINCTION

Adoption (26 Nov 1949) is distinct from commencement (26 Jan 1950).

TRAP TESTED

Option B tests wrong date. Confuses adoption with Republic Day.

SOURCE

Fundamental Rights: Art. 12-35View sourcearrow_outward
play_arrowFull study note

The preamble specifies adoption on 26 Nov 1949, while major governance provisions took effect on 26 Jan 1950 to honor the 1930 Purna Swaraj declaration.
