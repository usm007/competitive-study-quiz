The Fundamental Rights in Part III entered into force on:

check CORRECT B. 26 January 1950

WHY THIS IS RIGHT

Part III rights became enforceable on 26 January 1950, the date the Constitution commenced — not the adoption date (26 November 1949).

TRAP TESTED

Option A tests wrong date.

SOURCE

1. Fundamental Rights: Articles 12 to 35, an overview · p.1 View source →

Historical Context: While certain transitional provisions relating to citizenship (Articles 5-9) and elections (Article 324) came into force immediately on 26 November 1949, Article 394 expressly provided that the remaining provisions including the entirety of Part III (Articles 12 through 35) came into force on the Republic Commencement Day: 26 January 1950.
